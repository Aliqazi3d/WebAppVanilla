🐾 Super Wildlife Hero Tips!

Leave water out! 🥣

Hedgehogs and birds love a shallow dish of clean water.

Make a log pile 🪵

Bugs hide in logs, and hedgehogs eat the bugs!

Keep it quiet 🤫

If you see a wild animal, watch from far away.

Tell a grown-up 📞

Found a hurt animal? Ask an adult to call us.
